About Me

I am a PhD student in the School of Computer Science at University College Dublin (UCD), working on Robust Machine Learning with an emphasis on Trustworthy and Responsible AI.

My research interests include predictive multiplicity, the Rashomon effect, and human–AI alignment.

Research

Research Interests

01

Predictive Multiplicity

Understanding situations where multiple machine-learning models achieve similar predictive performance but produce different predictions for individual cases.

02

Rashomon Effect

Studying the space of similarly performing models and what model multiplicity means for reliability, interpretation, and decision-making.

03

Human–AI Alignment

Exploring the relationship between model behaviour, ambiguity in human judgments, and the alignment of machine-learning systems with human decision-making.
