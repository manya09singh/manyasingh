About

About me

I am a PhD student in the School of Computer Science at University College Dublin (UCD). I previously completed a Master's degree in Mathematics and Statistics at UCD.

My doctoral research explores how multiple predictive models can achieve similar performance while differing in their behaviour, explanations, and implications for people. My current interests include predictive multiplicity, the Rashomon effect, and human–AI alignment.

Research

Research interests

Predictive Multiplicity

Studying the existence and consequences of multiple high-performing predictive models, and what model choice means when performance alone does not determine a unique solution.

Rashomon Effect

Investigating sets of similarly accurate models and the diversity of their predictions, explanations, and decision behaviour.

Human–AI Alignment

Exploring how predictive systems can better align with human goals, expectations, preferences, and decision-making contexts.
